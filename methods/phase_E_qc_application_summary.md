
## Independent verification and cohort-level pattern

Full site retention machine-verified directly against source files
(results/qc/phase_e_site_retention.tsv), exact match to all numbers above.

Retention rate differs systematically by cohort: South Africa 51-86%
retained per gene (mean ~76%), Europe 88-97% retained per gene (mean
~94%), with zero overlap across all 13 genes. This is expected given
sample size: South Africa (n=120) vs Europe (n=226) means the same
absolute number of low-quality genotypes represents a larger percentage
of missingness at any given site in the smaller cohort. This is a
consequence of applying a fixed percentage threshold across cohorts of
different size, not a South Africa-specific data quality issue. Worth
noting explicitly when comparing final SNP counts between cohorts.
