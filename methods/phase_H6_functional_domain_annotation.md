# Phase H6 — Functional and domain annotation of protein-altering candidates

## Objective

Phase H6 evaluates whether the 62 protein-altering candidate variants identified in Phase G occur within or overlap established functional, domain, motif, topology, or other biologically annotated regions of their corresponding proteins.

H6 provides an evidence stream complementary to:

- H2: population-level allele frequencies
- H3: breed-level allele frequencies
- H4: predicted functional impact of amino-acid substitutions
- H5: evolutionary conservation of candidate residues

H6 does not perform structural modelling and does not by itself establish that a variant is functionally damaging.

### Coordinate-transfer rule

External protein annotations must not be transferred to candidate residues solely on the basis of gene symbol or protein name.

Direct residue-coordinate transfer is permitted only when the external protein sequence is demonstrated to be identical to the protein sequence defining the candidate coordinates.

Where sequences are not identical, explicit sequence/coordinate reconciliation is required before external features can be assigned to candidate residues.

This safeguard is particularly important for CMAH because the candidate set contains two different protein coordinate systems.

## H6.1 — External protein reference mapping

UniProt is used as a protein-level annotation anchor where suitable bovine records are available. External protein records are evaluated against the exact bovine reference sequences used in the candidate analysis.

### GNE pilot

The Ensembl release 110 GNE reference was confirmed as:

- Ensembl protein: ENSBTAP00000001520
- length: 722 aa

A direct UniProt cross-reference query using the Ensembl protein identifier returned no record. A Bos taurus gene-specific UniProt search identified four GNE records of lengths 752, 722, 612 and 648 aa.

UniProt accession F1MIL9 was the 722-aa candidate record.

Direct sequence comparison demonstrated exact identity between ENSBTAP00000001520 and F1MIL9:

- Ensembl length: 722 aa

- exact sequence identity: TRUE
- SHA256 for both sequences:
  `0335ee408bf4202c4eeb0ce55c1cb5b1d328da903cca8258861a42af5db243d9`

Therefore direct residue-coordinate transfer between ENSBTAP00000001520 and UniProt F1MIL9 is valid.

The GNE pilot established the sequence-based mapping strategy to be applied systematically to the remaining H6 reference proteins.

The frozen Ensembl release 110 NEU1 reference is 416 aa, whereas reviewed bovine UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot A6BMK7 is 415 aa.

Pairwise reconciliation showed 411 identical residues among 415 aligned residues (99.04% identity), with the sequence differences concentrated around H5 positions 268–275.

Eight of nine NEU1 candidate residues mapped to identical residues in A6BMK7.

NEU1 L275 mapped to a gap in A6BMK7 and was therefore classified:

`UNMAPPED_GAP`

`NO_DIRECT_UNIPROT_FEATURE_TRANSFER`

This is consistent with the independent H5 comparative-sequence result in which NEU1 L275 had 0/16 non-gap comparative orthologues. This concordance is treated as evidence of reference/local sequence divergence and not as evidence that the variant is benign or damaging.

### H6.1 downstream annotation rule

The H6.1 mapping table functions as a gatekeeper for subsequent functional/domain annotation.

Downstream feature assignment must obey the `feature_transfer_status` field.

In particular:

- `DIRECT_TRANSFER_ALLOWED` permits direct coordinate transfer from the validated identical external sequence.
- `MAPPED_RESIDUE_TRANSFER_ALLOWED` permits transfer only through the explicitly reconciled external coordinate.
- `FEATURE_RECONCILIATION_ALLOWED` requires the individual external feature and its source accession to be reconciled before assignment.
- `NO_DIRECT_UNIPROT_FEATURE_TRANSFER` prohibits direct residue-specific UniProt feature assignment but does not prohibit separately validated exact-sequence-compatible domain/family evidence.
- `NO_RESIDUE_LEVEL_FEATURE_TRANSFER` prohibits ordinary residue-level feature transfer.

Mapping failure, isoform sensitivity, absence of a UniProt feature, or absence of a direct UniProt sequence equivalent must not be interpreted as evidence that a candidate is functionally benign.


## Evidence interpretation

H6 annotations will be interpreted as contextual functional evidence rather than proof of variant effect.

Evidence may include:

1. curated or experimentally grounded protein features;
2. established protein-family, domain, or motif annotations;
3. relevant topology or sequence-context annotations.

Absence of an annotated feature at a candidate position must not be interpreted as evidence that the variant is benign or functionally irrelevant.

## Exclusions

The preliminary-manuscript H6 analysis will not include:

- AlphaFold-based structural interpretation
- molecular docking
- molecular dynamics simulation
- de novo structural modelling
- unsupported claims that proximity to a functional region proves functional impact
