# Final Phase H Candidate Prioritisation Provenance

## Scope

Final candidate prioritisation was performed after completion and integration of
H2 population-frequency, H3 breed-frequency, H4 functional-impact, H5 evolutionary-
conservation, and H6 known protein/domain-context evidence.

The authoritative candidate universe remained all 62 protein-altering variants.
No candidate was removed from the master evidence matrix during prioritisation.

All 62 candidates were reviewed using the integrated H2-H6 evidence framework.
This review identified 15 serious contenders for detailed candidate-level
adjudication.

Final interpretation status:

- PRIMARY_SHORTLIST: 7
- RESERVE: 8
- NOT_SHORTLISTED: 47

NOT_SHORTLISTED indicates that a candidate was reviewed within the full integrated
evidence framework but did not advance into the final 15-candidate contender set.
It does not mean that the candidate was removed from the authoritative evidence
matrix or considered biologically irrelevant.

## Evidence hierarchy

Candidate interpretation used the following qualitative hierarchy:

1. Variant consequence and native bovine SIFT evidence
2. Evolutionary conservation from H5 Rate4Site analysis
3. Validated residue/protein/domain context from H6
4. Direct biological relevance within the bovine sialic-acid pathway
5. Current South African and European population/breed segregation as a secondary
   practical modifier
6. Complementary genome-wide population-genetic evidence to be integrated later
   as a separate evidence dimension

No arithmetic composite score or fixed numerical ranking formula was used.

## Interpretation rules

- Missing evidence was not treated as negative evidence.
- SIFT-unavailable variants were treated as missing functional-prediction evidence.
- H5 NOT_ASSESSABLE status was treated as missing conservation evidence.
- Protein-level H6 context was treated as lower-resolution context, not biological
  irrelevance.
- Negative Rate4Site values were interpreted comparatively as evidence of slower
  evolutionary change; no arbitrary high/moderate/low conservation thresholds
  were introduced.
- SIFT-tolerated variants were not automatically interpreted as functionally neutral.
- Discordance between SIFT and conservation was retained explicitly rather than
  averaged away.
- Overlapping annotation sources were not treated as independent evidence votes.
- Residue-mapping confidence was distinguished from direct functional-feature
  annotation.
- Stop-gained and stop-lost variants were treated as distinct consequence classes
  and were not forced into the same evidence structure as ordinary missense
  variants.

## Population-evidence refinement

Initial exploratory prioritisation gave current breed/population occurrence a
stronger practical role.

During final adjudication, this was refined because the planned DFG Aim 3 does not
depend solely on the presently analysed 16-breed discovery representation. Aim 3
will use targeted genotyping in a larger accessible cohort informed by broader
South African and 1000 Bull Genomes resources.

Accordingly, current population evidence was retained as a feasibility and
accessibility modifier rather than a hard inclusion/exclusion gate.

Population evidence was interpreted using the following distinctions:

- PRESENT with observed ALT: currently observed in the available cohort
- PRESENT with no observed ALT: site interrogated, ALT not detected in that cohort
- NOT_IN_SOURCE: site could not be evaluated in that source
- ABSENT_AFTER_QC: QC-limited evidence and not biological absence

No minimum allele-frequency or breed-count threshold was imposed for final
molecular prioritisation.

## Human adjudication

The automated provisional disposition was used only as an initial review scaffold.

All 62 candidates were considered within the integrated H2-H6 evidence framework.
Fifteen candidates showed sufficient molecular, biological, or practical evidence
to advance into detailed candidate-level adjudication.

From these 15 contenders, seven were designated PRIMARY_SHORTLIST and eight were
retained as RESERVE candidates.

The final primary shortlist contains:

- NEU3 F159C
- ST8SIA4 I73L
- ST8SIA2 R129Q
- NEU3 E94K
- CMAH N242S
- ST3GAL4 R203S
- CMAH I388M

The reserve set contains:

- NEU1 L275P
- CMAS E128*

The reserve set is retained as a biologically justified substitution pool for
later targeted genotyping and genotype-informed phenotypic validation.

## Independence safeguard

Previous publication history was not used as evidence for candidate prioritisation.

CMAH candidates were evaluated only using evidence generated or validated in the
present workflow, including coordinate validity, conservation, functional/context
evidence, pathway relevance, and current population evidence.

## Planned downstream integration

The seven primary candidates are computational priorities rather than an immutable
experimental panel.

Complementary genome-wide population structure, differentiation, and selection
results will later be incorporated as a separate evidence dimension.

For Aim 3, targeted genotyping in a larger accessible cohort will establish actual
genotype availability before genotype-informed early-lactation milk selection and
Neu5Ac, Neu5Gc, and polySia phenotyping.
