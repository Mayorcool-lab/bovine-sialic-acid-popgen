## 1. Scientific objective

H6.2 asks:

For each of the 62 validated protein-altering candidate variants, what known
protein, domain, motif, catalytic, binding, membrane, or other defensible
functional context can be assigned using curated or reproducible annotation
resources?

H6.2 is restricted to known functional/domain annotation.

No new structural modelling, AlphaFold interpretation, molecular docking,
molecular dynamics, or de novo mechanistic modelling is performed in this
phase.

Candidate-level annotation must respect the reference-coordinate and
feature-transfer rules established and frozen in H6.1.

---

## 2. Governing scientific rules

1. H6.1 mapping and feature-transfer status is authoritative.
2. H6.1 reference files are not modified by H6.2.
3. Candidate residue annotation is performed only on validated external
   coordinates.
4. H5 protein coordinates must not be substituted for external annotation
   coordinates where H6.1 established a mapped external residue.
5. UniProt and InterPro are complementary evidence layers.
6. Missing UniProt feature annotation is not interpreted as absence of
   functional/domain context.
7. InterPro and member-database entries are preserved by source database,
   accession, type, and coordinate interval.
8. Multiple overlapping InterPro/member-database entries are not treated as
   independent evidence votes or converted into a numerical evidence score.
9. Direct residue overlap is objective:
      feature_start <= candidate_position <= feature_end
10. No arbitrary "near active site", "near motif", or other distance threshold
    is introduced.
11. Raw ECO evidence codes are retained where supplied.
12. Absence of an ECO code does not invalidate the existence of the annotated
    feature.
13. Feature classes such as Chain and Signal are retained but are not treated
    as functionally equivalent to active sites, binding sites, motifs, or
    catalytic domains.
14. No naive arithmetic composite score is created in H6.2.
